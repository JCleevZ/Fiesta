Services

One unbroken cold chain.

Our Epping facility offers a fully integrated logistics and cold-chain platform, designed to move fresh produce efficiently and reliably through one of South Africa's most strategic export gateways. Located just 15km from the Port of Cape Town, we're built to support growers, exporters and shipping line partners with real capacity and hands-on operational control.

Solving Cape Town's Port Congestion

A fully utilised consolidation centre.

We're developing our Epping site into a fully utilised container consolidation centre — a facility purpose-built to ease pressure on the Cape Town port corridor and strengthen the broader fruit supply chain, for the benefit of growers, exporters and shipping line partners alike.

In October 2025, we hosted Cape Town Mayor Geordin Hill-Lewis at the facility to share this vision directly with the City.

Fleet & distribution

Minutes from the port.

Positioned in Epping Industria with direct access to port, road and market infrastructure, our readily available refrigerated fleet keeps the cold chain intact from our door to its destination.
