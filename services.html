What we do

Cold room + staging

Refrigerated storage and staging held to stringent market standards, with the capacity to receive, hold and prepare export and local-market produce under continuous cold-chain control.

Key advantages

Reliable, scalable, secure.

Reliable, scalable, secure logistics solutions. Our dedicated, full-time operational ground team ensures continuous oversight, high service standards and disciplined execution. This hands-on operational capacity allows Fiesta Fresh to offer customers a reliable, scalable and secure logistics solution aligned with modern fresh produce requirements.

Trading expertise. Our trading model is underpinned by a commitment to quality, reliability and responsiveness, backed by strong global partnerships and responsive execution. This ensures that customers receive fresh produce that meets stringent market and consumer requirements. Through market-led trading in locally sourced and imported fresh produce, we serve retail, independent customers and export markets.

Fleet & distribution

Minutes from the port.

Positioned in Epping Industria with direct access to port, road and market infrastructure, our readily available refrigerated fleet keeps the cold chain intact from our door to its destination.
