About us

A multi-faceted partner.

Serving local, regional and international markets — Fiesta Fresh was established as a dynamic fresh produce trading business, aimed at the evolving needs of modern food supply chains, leveraging local market insights and global capacity.

Our story

A trusted service our partners rely on.

At Fiesta Fresh, we deliver more than just fresh produce. We provide a trusted service that our partners can rely on. We leverage our experience along with comprehensive logistics solutions.

Our integrated approach not only enhances reliability and efficiency but also empowers our partners to grow confidently with us as their dedicated fresh produce marketer.

Our value proposition

Value beyond the transaction.

We offer a fully integrated logistics and cold-chain platform designed to support efficient, reliable movement of fresh produce through one of South Africa's most strategic export gateways.

Together, our trading expertise, infrastructure and operational capacity enable Fiesta Fresh to deliver integrated supply chain solutions that add value beyond the transaction — supporting efficiency, product integrity and long-term partnerships.

Our global reach

Backed by one of the world's largest fresh produce companies.

Through its majority shareholder, Fiesta Fresh leverages a global supply network backed by Dole plc, one of the world's largest and most established fresh produce companies.

This global reach provides access to a broad product portfolio, deep market intelligence and best-in-class operational expertise, enabling Fiesta Fresh to navigate complex supply environments with confidence.

The facility

A consolidation centre at the gateway.

Positioned in Epping Industria with direct access to port, road and market infrastructure, the facility operates as a fully utilised container consolidation centre — strengthening the fruit supply chain for growers, exporters and shipping-line partners alike.
