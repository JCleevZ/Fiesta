Our story

A trusted service our partners rely on.

Fiesta Fresh was established in 2024 as a dynamic fresh produce trading business, formed to meet the evolving needs of modern food supply chains by combining deep local market insight with global sourcing capacity.

We publicly launched in January 2025, backed by Dole Africa Holdings alongside a board of independent fresh produce industry specialists — giving us both the scale of a global player and the agility of a founder-led business.

Our value proposition

Value beyond the transaction.

We trade in both locally sourced and imported food products, supplying a diverse customer base that includes major retail chains, independent customers and export markets. Our trading model is underpinned by a commitment to quality, reliability and responsiveness — and supported by our own integrated cold-chain platform at Epping, one of South Africa's key export gateways.

We're building more than a supply relationship. We aim to deliver value beyond the transaction — helping our partners grow with confidence.

Our global reach

Backed by one of the world's largest fresh produce companies.

Through our majority shareholder, Dole Africa Holdings, Fiesta Fresh leverages a global network backed by Dole plc — one of the world's largest and most established fresh produce marketers, with a heritage stretching back to 1850, more than 38,500 employees and over 250 facilities worldwide.

That global backing means access to broader sourcing options, deeper market intelligence and operational expertise, applied locally through our Cape Town team.

The facility

A consolidation centre at the gateway.

Positioned in Epping Industria with direct access to port, road and market infrastructure, the facility operates as a fully utilised container consolidation centre — strengthening the fruit supply chain for growers, exporters and shipping-line partners alike.
